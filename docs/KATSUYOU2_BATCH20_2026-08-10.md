## Verification decision

For `うまれる`, past negative `うまれなかった` and conditional `うまれれば` are `valid`. Potential `うまれられる`, volitional `うまれよう`, and causative `うまれさせる` are `deferred`: beginner examples either require abstract counterfactual context, assign intention to a non-volitional event, or shift toward the lexical causative `産ませる`. The three deferred values are stored as `null` and excluded from quizzes.

All five advanced forms are `valid` for `おきる` and both source senses of `おしえる`. Examples keep teaching content separate from disclosing an address, phone number, or route. Causative examples identify the causer and the person performing the action.
