## Verification decision

All five advanced forms are `valid` for the first three verbs. For stative `かかる`, past negative and conditional are `valid`, potential is `not_applicable`, and volitional and causative are `deferred`.

The potential `おもえる` uses its natural judgment or impression reading rather than forcing an artificial physical-ability context. The causative `おわらせる` identifies the director, worker, task, and deadline because the same surface form is also established as a transitive expression.
