## Verification decision

All five advanced forms for `てつだう`, `とまる`, and `とる` were marked `valid` before example release.

For `なおる`, past negative `なおらなかった` and conditional `なおれば` are `valid`. Potential `なおれる` and volitional `なおろう` are `not_applicable` in this beginner curriculum. Causative `なおらせる` is `deferred`: the form can be constructed, but natural beginner contexts ordinarily use the transitive verb `なおす` (`なおそう`, and where needed `なおさせる`). These three forms are stored as `null` and excluded from quizzes.
