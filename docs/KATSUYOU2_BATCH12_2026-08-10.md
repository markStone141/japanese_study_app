## Scope

- なくす / 失くす（物を紛失する）
- ならう / 習う
- なる
- ぬぐ / 脱ぐ

## Verification decision

For `失くす`, past negative `なくさなかった` and conditional `なくせば` are valid in an explicit lost-property context. Potential `なくせる` and volitional `なくそう` ordinarily shift toward `無くす` (eliminate), so they are `deferred`. Causative `なくさせる` is also `deferred` because a beginner example for the misplacement sense would be contrived. All three are stored as `null` and excluded from quizzes. `無くす` is reserved for a separate future lexical entry.

All five advanced forms for `ならう`, `なる`, and `ぬぐ` were marked `valid` before example release. The causative `ならせる` uses an explicit person and assigned role to avoid confusion with `鳴らせる`.
