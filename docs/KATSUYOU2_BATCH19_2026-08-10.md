## Verification decision

For the existence and child-possession senses of `いる`, past negative `いなかった` and conditional `いれば` are `valid`. Potential `いられる`, volitional `いよう`, and causative `いさせる` are `deferred` because ordinary examples shift toward keeping, staying, or allowing someone to remain rather than preserving the source senses. The six deferred values are stored as `null` and excluded from quizzes by user decision.

All five advanced forms are `valid` for the stay sense of `いる` and for `いれる`. Their examples explicitly identify a place and duration or a container and inserted object so the intended meanings remain clear.
