## Verification decision

All five advanced forms of `もらう` and both senses of `やすむ` are marked `valid`.

For `やくにたつ`, past negative `やくにたたなかった`, potential `やくにたてる`, conditional `やくにたてば`, and volitional `やくにたとう` are released. Causative `やくにたたせる` is `deferred`, stored as `null`, and excluded from quizzes because ordinary beginner contexts normally use the separate transitive verb `やくだてる` / 役立てる.

The two `やすむ` entries keep physical rest and taking leave from work distinct in every advanced example.
