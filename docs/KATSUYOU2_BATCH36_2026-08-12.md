## Verification decision

All five advanced forms are `valid` for all four verbs.

The potential forms `おける` and `おくれる` overlap in sound with forms or words derived from other verbs. Their examples therefore state the object and destination or location explicitly, preserving the intended meanings “can put” and “can send.” Causative examples identify who directs or permits the action, who performs it, and the affected object.
