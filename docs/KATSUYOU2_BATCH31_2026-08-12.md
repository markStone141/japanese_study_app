## Verification decision

Five advanced forms are `valid` for 残業する, する（do/play）, and 修理する. For する（put on a tie）, past negative, conditional, volitional, and causative are `valid`; its potential is `deferred` because `ネクタイができる` does not uniquely preserve the source wearing sense in a standalone beginner cloze.
